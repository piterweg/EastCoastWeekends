The Finger Lakes, if you have three days

This is the longest trip on the site: four and a half hours by car. Go for two or three nights, not one.

Why go

The long-weekend exception

The Finger Lakes reward time: wine trails around Seneca and Cayuga, the Watkins Glen gorge and Ithaca's gorges and food.

A car is essential. Base in Watkins Glen, Geneva or Ithaca. Two nights is the minimum; three lets you do wine one day and the gorge and Ithaca the next.

What to do

Three anchors

One gorge, one wine trail, one lake town.

Gorge

Watkins Glen State Park

The gorge trail is the reason many people come. Go early; it is busy in summer.

The plan

Two nights, two full days

Drive Friday, gorge Saturday, wine Sunday, home Monday if you can.

01 Friday

Drive

Four and a half hours, check in, early dinner. Keep the evening short.

  • Dinner — Watkins Glen or Geneva
02 Saturday

Gorge and lake

Questions

__FAQ_TITLE__

Can I do it without a car?

Not practically. Fly to Rochester or Ithaca and rent a car if you do not want to drive from New York.

How many nights?

Two minimum, three better. The drive is four and a half hours each way.

When is best?

Summer for the lake and early autumn for wine and foliage.
