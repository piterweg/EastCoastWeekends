Why go

The easy beach weekend

NJ Transit runs direct buses from Port Authority to Atlantic City. The boardwalk, the beach and the casinos are all within walking distance of the drop-off.

It is not the prettiest town on the shore, but it is the easiest: no car, no parking, and enough indoor options if the weather turns. Steel Pier and the Absecon Lighthouse are the non-casino anchors.

Questions

__FAQ_TITLE__

Do I need a car?

No. The bus drops you within walking distance of the boardwalk.

Is one night enough?

Yes for a beach and boardwalk weekend; two nights if you want a slower Sunday.

When is best?

Summer for swimming; late spring and early autumn for cheap rates.
