Washington in a weekend

Under three hours by train, then the Mall, the Smithsonian and a city that rewards an early start.

Why go

The museum capital

The Smithsonian museums are free, which changes the maths: you can dip in for an hour and leave without guilt.

Stay near the Mall or Dupont Circle. The train arrives at Union Station; the Metro gets you to the Mall in minutes. Book Capitol tours in advance and keep one evening free for the monuments, which are best after dark.

What to do

Three things to prioritise

The Mall, one museum and the monuments at night.

Museums

Smithsonian museums

Free entry. Pick two: Air and Space, Natural History or American History. Do not try all three.

Check opening hours
Capitol

US Capitol tour

Free but timed; book ahead. The Library of Congress is next door and worth an hour.

Book through the official site
Evening

Monuments at night

Walk from Lincoln to the Washington Monument after dark — quieter, cooler and better lit.

Start at Lincoln
The plan

A compact two days

Museums on Saturday, monuments and Georgetown on Sunday.

01 Saturday

The Mall

Capitol tour in the morning, one Smithsonian before lunch, a second after. Walk the Mall at sunset and eat near Penn Quarter.

  • Capitol tour — Capitol Visitor Center
  • Air and Space — Independence Ave
  • Penn Quarter dinner — 7th Street
02 Sunday

Monuments and Georgetown

Early walk around the monuments, then Georgetown for brunch and the canal. Train home late afternoon.

  • Lincoln Memorial — West end of the Mall
  • Georgetown — M Street
  • Union Station — Amtrak home

Questions

__FAQ_TITLE__

Is a weekend enough?

Yes for the Mall, two museums and the monuments. The city rewards a second visit, but two days give a real sense of it.

Are museums really free?

The Smithsonian museums are free. Special exhibitions and some tours are ticketed.

Where should I stay?

Near the Mall for sightseeing, Dupont Circle for restaurants and a quieter evening.
