Philadelphia in two days

The closest big-city weekend: history in the morning, market lunch, a museum afternoon and a walk along the river.

Why go

The most walkable weekend on the East Coast

Center City is compact and flat: Independence Hall, the market and the museum district are all within a long walk or a short bus ride.

Amtrak from Penn Station puts you at 30th Street in about an hour; NJ Transit plus SEPTA is slower and much cheaper. Stay in Center City and you will not need a car or a subway pass. The one thing to book ahead is Independence Hall, which is timed and free.

What to do

What actually matters

Two days is enough for the old city and one museum — not everything.

History

Independence Hall

Timed, free tickets from the visitor centre. The Liberty Bell is next door and needs no ticket.

Book the timed slot
Food

Reading Terminal Market

A covered market with dozens of counters. Go hungry and go early; it is busiest at lunch.

Allow an hour
Museum

Philadelphia Museum of Art

The art is excellent and the steps are the photo. Allow three hours, or choose the Barnes instead.

Check opening days
The plan

One full day plus one morning

Arrive Friday night or early Saturday; leave Sunday afternoon.

01 Saturday

Old City and the market

Independence Hall first (timed ticket), then the Liberty Bell and a walk through Old City. Lunch at Reading Terminal Market, afternoon at the museum, dinner in Center City.

  • Independence Hall — Old City
  • Reading Terminal Market — 12th and Arch
  • Museum of Art — Benjamin Franklin Parkway
02 Sunday

Rittenhouse and the river

Brunch in Rittenhouse, a walk along the Schuylkill, then the train home. If it rains, swap in the Barnes Foundation.

  • Rittenhouse Square — Center City
  • Schuylkill River Trail — Behind the museum
  • 30th Street Station — Amtrak home

Questions

__FAQ_TITLE__

Is one night enough?

Yes. One full Saturday and a Sunday morning covers the old city and one museum.

Amtrak or NJ Transit?

Amtrak is faster and more comfortable; NJ Transit to Trenton plus SEPTA is slower but can be much cheaper.

Where should I stay?

Center City, near Rittenhouse or Market East. Both are walkable to the old city and the market.
