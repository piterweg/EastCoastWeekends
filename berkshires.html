Three and a half hours by train (once a day) or under three by car: museums, music and a countryside that feels further away than it is.

Why go

Culture in the hills

The Berkshires pack a surprising amount into a small area: Mass MoCA in North Adams, Tanglewood in summer, and Great Barrington for food and shops.

The Lake Shore Limited runs once a day to Pittsfield, so the train works if you plan around it. A car makes the towns and trailheads much easier. Base in Great Barrington or Lenox.

Questions

__FAQ_TITLE__

Train or car?

The train is scenic but once daily; a car makes the towns and trails much easier.

Is two nights enough?

Yes for Mass MoCA and one town. Add a night for Tanglewood or a longer hike.

When is Tanglewood?

Summer only; check the season dates and book ahead.
