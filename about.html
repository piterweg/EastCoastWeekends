About

Weekends, planned honestly

East Coast Weekends is a small independent guide to short trips from New York. No sponsored rankings, no ten-stop checklists, no invented fares.

IndependentTrain-firstFares checked November 2026

How we choose destinations

A place makes the site if the journey leaves enough of the weekend to enjoy it. We start with the train timetable, then build the two days around what is actually open, then check the cost of the trip end to end.

How we check fares and hours

Train times and fares come from the operator's own timetable and booking pages; opening hours come from the venue. Prices are the adult "from" rates checked on the date shown on each guide. Dynamic pricing means the fare at checkout can be higher.

Independence

No venue or hotel pays to appear. When we link to an official ticket page it is because that is the correct place to book, not because we earn a commission. If that changes, the disclosure will be on the page.

Corrections

If a train time, fare or opening hour has changed, tell us and we will fix it. The checked date at the top of each guide is the date the facts were last verified.
