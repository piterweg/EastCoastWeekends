The Hudson Valley, without a car

Dia Beacon, Storm King and a river town in one weekend — all reachable on the Metro-North Hudson Line.

Why go

The easiest weekend out of New York

The Hudson Line runs along the river from Grand Central to Beacon and beyond. The trip is short enough to leave on Saturday morning and be in a gallery by lunch.

Beacon is the practical base: the station, Dia Beacon and the main street are within walking distance. Storm King is a short taxi or bus ride away and is the reason many people come. Cold Spring, one stop south, is the other half of the weekend: a main street, a waterfront and a trailhead.

What to do

Three things that justify the trip

Pick two. Trying to do all three makes it a driving weekend, not a train one.

Art

Dia Beacon

A converted factory full of large-scale contemporary art, a short walk from the station. Allow two to three hours.

Check opening days
Sculpture

Storm King Art Center

An open-air sculpture park in New Windsor. Taxi or seasonal shuttle from Beacon; allow half a day and check the season.

Book a timed slot
Town

Cold Spring

One main street, a waterfront and a short uphill walk with river views. Best on the way home.

Walk the waterfront
The plan

Saturday and Sunday

One art stop per day, with the town in between.

01 Saturday

Beacon

Arrive by mid-morning, drop bags, then Dia Beacon before lunch. Afternoon on Main Street: coffee, bookshops, the riverfront. Dinner in Beacon.

  • Metro-North from Grand Central — Hudson Line to Beacon
  • Dia Beacon — 10 minutes on foot from the station
  • Main Street — Coffee and dinner
02 Sunday

Storm King and Cold Spring

Taxi to Storm King early, then back to Beacon and one stop to Cold Spring for the waterfront walk before the train home.

  • Storm King Art Center — Taxi from Beacon
  • Cold Spring waterfront — One stop south
  • Metro-North home — Cold Spring station

Questions

__FAQ_TITLE__

Do I need a car?

No. Beacon is walkable from the station and Storm King is a short taxi ride. A car makes Storm King easier but is not essential.

Is one night enough?

Yes if you pick two of the three stops. Two nights let you add a longer walk and a slower Sunday.

When is the best time?

Late spring and autumn for walking. Summer is green and busy; winter closes some sculpture parks.
