Planning

One night or two?

The number of nights should follow the journey, not the other way round. Under two hours each way, one night is enough. Over three, one night is usually wasted.

How to decide

Three questions

01

What is the journey?

Under two hours each way: one night. Over three: two nights, or a different destination.

02

What do you want to do?

One anchor (a museum, a beach, a trail) fits a night. Two anchors need two nights.

03

Is there a car?

Without a car, stay where the station is. With a car, the spread-out places open up.

Questions

Weekend length questions

Is one night ever enough for Boston?

Technically yes, but the travel eats the weekend. Two nights is the honest answer.

Can Niagara be a day trip?

No. The train is about nine hours each way; two nights is the minimum.

What if I only have one night?

Stay under two hours: Hudson Valley, Philadelphia, Jersey Shore or Atlantic City.
