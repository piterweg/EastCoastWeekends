How to choose

Match the weekend to your energy

A long train with a short stay is worse than a short train with a long stay.

◆

If you only have one night

Stay under two hours each way: Hudson Valley or Philadelphia. Both leave you a full Saturday afternoon and most of Sunday.

◆

If you have two nights

Boston and Washington become comfortable rather than rushed. Niagara needs two nights as a minimum.

◆

If you are travelling by train

Book Amtrak early: the cheapest fares disappear first. For Philadelphia, NJ Transit plus SEPTA is slower but much cheaper.

◆

If the weather turns

Hudson Valley has Dia Beacon and Storm King; Philadelphia has museums; Boston has the MFA and Isabella Stewart Gardner.

Questions

Weekend questions

Do I need a car?

No. Every destination here can be done by train, with local buses or taxis for the last mile. Hudson Valley and Philadelphia are the easiest without one.

When should I book?

Amtrak fares rise as trains fill, so book two to six weeks ahead for the best price. Hotels are cheapest outside leaf-peeping weekends and school holidays.

Is one night enough for Boston or Washington?

It is possible but tight. Two nights turn either into a proper weekend rather than a long day with a hotel stay.
