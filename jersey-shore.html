The Jersey Shore by train

An hour fifty from Penn Station and you are on the boardwalk. The easiest summer weekend on this list.

Why go

The beach, without a car

NJ Transit's North Jersey Coast Line runs from Penn Station to Asbury Park and beyond. The station is a short walk from the boardwalk and the beach.

Asbury Park has the boardwalk, the Stone Pony and the restaurants; Ocean Grove next door is quieter and dry (no alcohol sales). Spring Lake is further south and prettier still. In summer, book the hotel early.

Questions

__FAQ_TITLE__

Do I need a car?

No. The train station is a short walk from the beach and boardwalk.

Which town is best?

Asbury Park for food and music; Ocean Grove for quiet; Spring Lake for a prettier, pricier beach.

When is the season?

Memorial Day to Labor Day for swimming; the boardwalk is open year-round.
