When to go, by season

The East Coast changes more than the postcards suggest. The beach is a summer trip; the hills are an autumn one; the cities work all year.

In practice

Three seasonal rules

Spring

Go south first

Washington and Philadelphia warm up earlier than the mountains.

Summer

Book the shore in May

Jersey Shore and Cape May weekends sell out by early summer.

Autumn

Chase the foliage north

The Hudson Valley and Berkshires peak before the city does.

Questions

Seasonal questions

When is the cheapest weekend?

Late autumn and winter outside holidays, especially in the cities. The shore is cheapest in shoulder season but many places close.

When is leaf season?

Usually late September to early November, moving south as the weeks pass. Check local foliage reports before booking.

Is summer a good time for the cities?

It is hot and busy. If you go, plan museums and early mornings; save the walking for spring or autumn.
